Our concept

Homemade food, closer to home.

CloseCook is a proposed privacy-conscious hyperlocal marketplace that helps housing-society residents discover and pre-order home-style meals from nearby home chefs.

The problem to investigate

Finding trusted local meals can take effort.

Residents may rely on restaurant apps, tiffin services, neighbourhood groups or personal networks. CloseCook proposes a more structured way to discover menus and coordinate pickup—but the frequency and severity of this problem still need primary research.

The concept must validate customer demand, chef participation, food-safety readiness, repeat ordering and viable economics before any real launch.

Initial target market

Mumbai housing societies

Potential customers are residents seeking convenient home-style meals. Potential supply partners are nearby home chefs who want local reach and control over the quantity and timing of orders.

  • Start with one society.
  • Use a simple menu and pre-order flow.
  • Test scheduled pickup before building delivery logistics.
  • Collect consent-based feedback and real order data before scaling.
What makes the concept different

Designed around a local community.

The proposed differentiation is the combination of local discovery, advance ordering and privacy-conscious handover—not a claim of proven market advantage.

01

Society-first discovery

Focus on a small nearby community before attempting city-wide coverage.

02

Predictable pickup

Use agreed windows and a designated collection point to reduce coordination friction.

03

Privacy-conscious flow

Limit unnecessary exposure of personal addresses and contact details.

Validation roadmap

Learn before scaling.

These are planned steps, not completed milestones.

1

Discover

Interview residents and home chefs about needs, trust, pricing and capacity.

2

Prototype

Test a menu and order-flow mock-up with potential users.

3

Check readiness

Review compliance, hygiene, payments, pickup and complaints before sales.

4

Run a small pilot

Only if justified, measure genuine orders, repeat intent and chef feasibility.

Evidence status: concept stage

No live marketplace, completed customer study, verified chef listings or completed food-safety checks are claimed by this demo.

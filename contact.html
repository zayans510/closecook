What we'd like to learn

Real feedback comes before a real launch.

Future research would explore how people currently find home-style meals, what makes a chef trustworthy, which prices feel reasonable and how pickup could work in a housing society.

  • Residents: ordering habits, needs, trust and pricing.
  • Home chefs: capacity, availability, order limits and fees.
  • Society representatives: pickup arrangements and community rules.

Frequently asked questions

Before you explore.

Can I order food on this website?

No. This is a concept prototype for an academic presentation. No orders or payments are processed.

Are the chef profiles real?

No. Names, profiles, dishes and prices are fictional examples created to demonstrate a possible user experience.

Has CloseCook launched?

No. The idea requires primary research, compliance review and a controlled validation plan before any live service.
